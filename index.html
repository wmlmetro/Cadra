How It Works

Four Simple Steps

From submitting your request to resolution — here's what to expect.

1

Choose a Service

Select the service domain that matches your issue — building, fleet, security or more.

2

Describe the Issue

Fill in the request form with details, your location, and attach any relevant photos.

3

Get Assigned

Your request is routed to the right team and you receive a confirmation with a tracking number.

4

Track & Resolve

Follow progress in real-time, communicate with the assigned team, and confirm resolution.
