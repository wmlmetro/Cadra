02 / Automated lifecycle

From purchase order to disposal.

Cadra creates the asset record as soon as the PO is raised. Delivery, tagging, moves, repairs and retirement are then added to that same record, with approvals where you require them.

  1. 01procured
    PO raised
    Vendra or SAP sends the PO, and Cadra creates asset records before delivery.
    connector.vendra
  2. 02received
    Goods received
    Staff scan the delivery against the PO. Nothing is typed in twice.
    station.receive
  3. 03tagged
    Tag printed
    The station prints a barcode and QR label in your agency’s tag format.
    station.print
  4. 04deployed
    Assigned
    The asset is linked to a room and a custodian and joins the active register.
    asset.deployed
  5. 05in_service
    Maintained
    Repairs, transfers, costs and downtime are added to its history.
    request.*
  6. 06disposed
    Retired
    Disposal goes through approval. The asset’s history is kept.
    approval.disposal

04 / Barcode station

Print the tag when the goods arrive.

The barcode station sits at the receiving desk. Staff scan the delivery against the purchase order, print a barcode and QR label, and stick it on the item. From then on, one scan brings up the asset's full record.

  1. 01
    Barcode and QR on every tag
    Tag layouts follow each agency’s numbering format.
  2. 02
    Print when goods are received
    Items are tagged as they come in, not weeks later.
  3. 03
    Scan to act
    Scan once to transfer an item, report a fault or see its history.
  4. 04
    Room-by-room audit
    Scan a room to see what is missing, misplaced or unexpected.

05 / Service requests

Staff report problems on WhatsApp or email. Cadra logs them.

Cadra reads each message, matches it to an asset and a room, opens a ticket with an SLA and replies in the same thread. If the match is uncertain, it asks the sender instead of guessing.
